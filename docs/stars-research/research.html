What the scientific record supports

A body of research supports parts of the research strategy: closed-loop interfaces with cultured neurons, learning-associated astrocytic participation in some animal memory processes, spinal cell maturation models, microelectrode acquisition and controlled physical connectivity. The combination proposed here remains unverified.

Five critical readings

R1 / 2022

Closed-loop embodied neurons

Kagan et al. demonstrated learning-like performance improvement in Pong-linked cultured neurons under a structured feedback setup. This supports interface feasibility, not the entire ChristEye learning theory.

Read Neuron article ↗
R2 / 2026

Organoids and retention limits

Robbins et al. studied feedback-driven cart-pole learning in mouse cortical organoids and reported improvement that did not persist over a 45-minute rest. This motivates a separate retention gate.

Read Cell Reports ↗
R3 / 2025

Astrocytes and recall

Williamson et al. reported learning-associated hippocampal astrocytic ensembles influencing recall in mice, with an NFIA-related mechanism.

Read Nature ↗
R4 / 2025

Multiday astrocytic trace

Dewa et al. reported experience-related astrocytic priming linked to memory stabilization. A priming trace is not the same claim as specific stored content.

Read Nature ↗
R10 / vendor

Reference MEA hardware

MaxWell Biosystems documents the MaxOne platform and its capabilities; it remains a candidate pending technical and service confirmation for this proposed assembly.

View platform ↗

Research order and deliverables

W0–W1Freeze the theory

Definitions, encoded task, claims matrix and data contracts.

W1–W2Qualify the instrument

Procurement, cell-free signals, timing, interlocks and source plans.

R1Prove one learner

Independent preparations, learning, delayed capability and repeatability.

R2–R3Test added roles

Observer, support and teacher benefit with isolation and costs.

The design declares no completed living-device build, training success, recovered astroengram, validated medical application or energy advantage. Confirmation requires repeatable results on independent preparations under pre-registered thresholds, source records, controller logs and matched comparisons.

Oversight, ethics and open science

A later laboratory program needs institutional biosafety review, source and consent documentation, supplier-specific operating limits, appropriate tissue-handling practices and a separate discussion of ethical oversight for living neural preparations. This public site provides research architecture and analysis plans; it does not provide a live-tissue culture protocol, unapproved stimulation settings, therapeutic claims or instructions for bypassing controls.

Publish negative results, invalid trials, dropout counts, changed definitions, controls, provenance hashes, task codebooks and the declared evidence level. For computing comparisons, evaluate a software classifier and fixed support policy with the same information and service costs. Any external claim should say precisely which preparation, outcome and horizon it covers.
