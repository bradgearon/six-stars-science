01 / ChristEye technical documentation

An instrument with separable intelligence.

The device is designed as a collection of independently supported, measurable systems joined by permissioned information routes—not as an undifferentiated living brain.

RESEARCH DESIGN / v0.4NONCLINICALUPDATED 13 NOV 2026
Implementation and drawings: the six engineering sheets, interface contracts and setup checklist are now available in the field manual.

System overview

Revision 0.4 proposes a modular learning instrument using commercial high-density microelectrode arrays (HD-MEAs). A core learner (C) is embedded in a controlled task environment. A separately trained observer (O) receives selected information about the core and learns a specific monitoring task. A later support-policy community (S) may propose responses. The host (H) translates signals, the evaluator (E) holds protected targets, and the independent limit controller (L) constrains physical actions.

Biological cartridges are independently supported and identified. A digital bridge relays coded information; it is not a synapse, axon, fluid bridge, or evidence of transferred memory.

Trust boundary. The biological observer cannot directly change fluid composition, drive a pump, rewrite its own decoder, or change stimulation limits. The evaluator protects held-out outcomes. An independent controller can refuse a proposal even when the host predicts benefit.

Staged implementation

R0 — Cell-free integration

Implement task generation, software-only simulation, event logs, permissions, sensory encoding, decoder freezing, timing and hardware fault tests. Demonstrate stimulation inhibit and data reproducibility with a nonliving load before any tissue use.

R1 — One characterized living core

Qualify source, viability, physiology, evoked responses and repeatable task acquisition. Test the core alone and after delays. No observer or support benefit is credited in this phase.

R2 — Two independent cartridges

Study C + O monitoring or A + B teacher/recipient experiments in distinct campaigns. Evaluate observer prediction against digital alternatives, and evaluate B alone after disconnecting A.

R3 — Support learner

Add S only after a useful observer channel has been established. S begins in shadow mode, then is compared with fixed policies. A fourth module is an expansion option rather than a first procurement assumption.

Marrow, lineage and cell composition

“Marrow” is an organizing role for provenance, foundation and developmental history. It does not mean inserting literal bone marrow into a spinal neural network. The proposed source is a traceable human iPSC line or well-characterized differentiated lots, with a partner-controlled neural production protocol and independent identity and function checks.

Soil: the receiving environment

The physical Soil includes a validated matrix, geometry, local environment, media composition, neighboring populations, fluid motion and exposure history. The informational Soil includes context, cue distributions and task consequences. These share provenance but must remain distinct variables. Proposed materials—laminin, PDL/PLO, BrainPhys-type activity-supporting medium and specified supplements—are candidates for qualification, not a universal culture recipe or a prescription to combine them.

Document inlet and local measurements separately. A reservoir pH reading is not an in situ electrode-level observation; likewise, medium composition cannot be inferred from firing rate. Compatibility, surface adsorption, gas exchange, evaporation, shear and analyte recovery must be measured before interpreting learning effects.

Electronic bridge: explicit routing contract

Every permitted route has a source, recipient, direction, bandwidth, freshness interval, codebook version, permissions and disconnect mechanism. C sends selected measured features to H; H encodes qualified observations for O; O’s frozen output becomes a candidate status; S may later propose a response. L independently checks physical validity.

The full separation test clears queued events, cached predictions, teacher-dependent normalization and shared adaptive state. Simple disconnection at the user interface does not establish recipient independence. Physical axonal joining belongs to a later, separately controlled revision requiring evidence beyond common drive, diffusion and cross talk.
