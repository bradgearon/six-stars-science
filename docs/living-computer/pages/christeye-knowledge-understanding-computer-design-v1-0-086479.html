CHRISTEYE KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING COMPUTER

Living Neural Substrate Research Design

Technical research specification | Version 1.0 | 13 November 2026

Scientific Scope and Claim Boundaries

This document specifies a staged, nonclinical research design for a biohybrid knowledge and understanding system. It is not a recipe for a patient product, not a human dosing recommendation, and not proof of any therapeutic effect. No cells, substrates, or networks described here should be swallowed, injected, implanted, or used outside approved laboratory oversight.

The design builds on the ChristEye Living Table & Energy Framework, Sylph Cancer Surveillance Concept, Experimental Design v1.1, Physical Learning Device concept, and related specifications. Framework terms (Soul, Lotus, Milk, Honey, Roots, Winds, Heart, Tree, Astroengram) are used as functional organizing vocabulary. They do not substitute for biological identification, measurements, or evidence.

Primary research question: Does a contained, closed-loop trained living neural network (or modular network of networks) improve detection, classification, retention, and controlled response to predefined sensor patterns beyond an ordinary software controller, without increasing nonspecific activity or harming the culture?

System Architecture

The system is partitioned into independent, evaluable modules so that learning, joining, and contribution can be measured separately.

ModuleRole and constraints
Observational / sensor networkSeparate cartridge or fluidically restricted compartment. Trained closed-loop on encoded normal / injury / malignant-pattern signals. Produces clear decision output. Astrocyte participation monitored.
Core understanding / main meshPrimary living learner on HD-MEA (planar culture or organoid). Receives observational output as one input among others. Trained for task performance, retention, and transfer.
Support / monitoring communitiesAdditional neuronal and astrocytic populations. Stabilize, route, or report. Joined first electronically, later by controlled axon-permissive paths. Independently evaluated.
Host computerTask oracle, encoding, frozen evaluation readout, independent limit controller, logging, and resource gating. Does not replace the living substrate.

Cell Lineages (Marrow)

All lineages start from a qualified iPSC bank with documented identity and genomic stability. Differentiation uses established dual-SMAD inhibition, regional patterning (RA + SHH pathway for spinal identity), and neurotrophic support. “Crown and Lotus machinery” and “embryo creation” map exclusively to controlled embryoid-body, neural-organoid, or patterned-progenitor generation. Full viable embryos are neither required nor proposed.

Observational network lineage

Dual-SMAD inhibition to neural progenitors / neuroepithelium.

Optional cortical or mixed regionalization.

Differentiation to glutamatergic and GABAergic neurons + astrocytes, or cortical organoids plated on HD-MEA.

Precedent: DishBrain closed-loop (Kagan et al. 2022); organoid cartpole learning (Robbins et al., Cell Reports 2026).

Core mesh lineage

Neural progenitors patterned with retinoic acid + SHH pathway agonist for ventral spinal identity (OLIG2 → ISL1/ChAT).

Co-culture with spinal-patterned astrocytes (Taga et al. 2019 precedent for accelerated maturation on MEA).

Optional interneuron populations for diversity.

Support lineages

Additional astrocyte lots (spinal or cortical) and selected interneurons with optical reporters.

Identity, viability, morphology, and electrophysiological release criteria before any joining.

Soil, Milk, and Honey Conditions

Soil (receiving context)

Qualified ECM (PDL/PLO + laminin ± fibronectin or Matrigel) on the electrode surface. BrainPhys-class medium + SM1/N2 + BDNF/GDNF + ascorbic acid. Monitor osmolality, pH, glucose/lactate, and ion balance. Exchange schedule is platform-qualified.

Milk (support / raising capacity)

Qualified medium, trophic factors, and metabolic support. Nourishment is never withheld as reward or punishment. Basic life support is enforced by the independent controller regardless of task score.

Honey (selection / attraction / stabilization)

Structured closed-loop stimulation and outcome-contingent feedback that preferentially stabilizes useful response patterns. For the observational network this is detection-contingent feedback. For support networks it is feedback tied to measured contribution to the main mesh. No bulk chemical is treated as automatic Honey or as a memory code.

Training Processes

Observational network training

Encode sensor patterns (normal / injury / malignant-associated feature vectors) as validated electrical stimulation profiles (rate and place coding, artifact-masked, within locked limits).

Define output regions decoded into a decision (alert, hold, or class).

Deliver structured feedback: predictable low-cost stimuli for correct early detection; higher-cost or less predictable stimuli for misses or false alarms.

Include rest / consolidation periods.

Freeze the evaluation readout. Score on held-out patterns against untrained, noncontingent-feedback, and pure-software baselines.

Success requires incremental classification, calibration, stability, and retention. A negative result is informative. Astrocyte Ca or reporter signals are correlated with task-related ensembles; selective contribution requires delayed, controlled tests.

Core mesh training

Closed-loop embodiment in task environments (DishBrain-style or organoid cartpole-style). Feedback contingent on performance. Retention and transfer tested after delay with frozen readout.

Joining Processes

Stage 1 — Electronic bridge (preferred first)

Logged, directionally controllable, delay-capable pathway from observational-network output to main-mesh input electrodes. Disconnectable without tissue disturbance. During teaching the bridge is active; for isolation tests it is removed, cached outputs cleared, and readout frozen. Compare against yoked/shuffled signals and matched software teaching.

Stage 2 — Physical axon-permissive joining

Microfluidic or PDMS microchannel geometries, or axon-bundle connections between organoids (Osaki et al. 2024 precedent). Route-specific measurements distinguish axonal communication from chemical coupling, common drive, or leakage. Independent observation access for each compartment. Later, separately gated extension.

Support / child networks

Generated from matched progenitor bank under the same patterning, matured separately. “Crown decision in embryo creation” maps to the choice of differentiation factors and plating geometry. Support networks receive feedback contingent on measured contribution to the main mesh so their activity patterns become useful. Evaluation remains independent.

Control Method for Support Networks

Host maintains a NeedRequest / resource-gate record (missing relation, observations, confidence, deadline, allowed recipients).

Support networks receive encoded requests or state summaries and produce candidate responses.

Lotus-style host coordinator compares predicted utility against resource reserves and protected capabilities (ACT / HOLD / UNRESOLVED).

Outcome-contingent structured stimulation increases the probability of useful support patterns within locked electrical and metabolic limits.

Independent Heart/safety controller can veto any action that violates operating bounds or protected old relations.

All interventions are reversible electronic routing first. Structural changes require separate gates.

Evidence Gates and Evaluation

Follow a staged G1–G6 structure specialized for multi-network work:

G1–G3: Source release, maturation, and interface competence for each lineage separately.

Observational training and retention must pass before joining.

Electronic teaching bridge and isolation tests.

Support-network contribution measured against fixed-schedule and non-adaptive controls.

Resource cost, old-task retention, and failure modes reported.

Optical or molecular assays only when required for the specific claim.

Efficiency is whole-device (host + acquisition + stimulation + support + storage). No energy advantage is assumed. Negative results are valuable and must be reported.

Safety, Independent Stop Rules, and Non-Claims

Independent stop rules and blinded outcome measurements apply to every arm. Any unanticipated growth, toxicity, instrument malfunction, or unreviewed methodological change triggers pause and review. Neuronal signals can encourage growth in some tumors; this possibility is tested explicitly where relevant. No version is self-administered or tested in a person outside a formally approved program.

Reasonable research claim: The system investigates adaptive biological pattern processing with longitudinal personalization potential, neuronal and astrocytic signal handling, and regulated support modules under independent safety control.

Not established: Daily or repeated dosing growing functional networks in a person; automatic identification of all cancer-related damage; reliable tumor killing by neural cells; prevention or cure of disease; safe lifelong integration; transferable Astroengrams as molecular packets; or superiority over software on any clinical endpoint.

Open Parameters and Next Deliverables

Cell densities, exact ratios, stimulation envelopes, optical assays, maturation criteria, and microchannel dimensions remain platform- and culture-qualified items. They are locked before live operation depends on them.

Subsystem compatibility matrix.

Supplier-confirmed parts list (HD-MEA platform, qualified iPSC sources, medium formulation).

Source and maturation acceptance criteria.

Interface drawings for electronic bridge and optional physical joining.

Pre-registered analysis plan with held-out sets, negative controls, and missing-data rules.

Document status: Proposed research architecture. Any use of human cells requires institutional review, biosafety assessment, and applicable regulatory authorization. This specification does not assert demonstrated knowledge extraction, understanding transfer, or therapeutic benefit.
