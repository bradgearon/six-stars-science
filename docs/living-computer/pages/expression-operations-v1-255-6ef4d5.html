R.1 Purpose and source provenance

Release 1.255 and engine 2.16 turn the inventor’s two proposed expression families into an explicit internal executor. Brad Gearon supplied the terms Slay and Splendor, clarified Slay as humility and grace, and requested expressions across the framework’s layers. The assistant identified the wording in 2 Thessalonians 2:8, confirmed contextual source records through the bundled ChristEye corpus, proposed the engineering contract and authored the code and checks. The record does not attribute the software implementation to an unassisted autonomous discovery.

build_expression_registry retains seven contextual passage records from 2 Thessalonians 2:3–8 and James 4:6, a pinned edition record and their digests. SourceRuntime.begin_expression_cycle also verifies the separate covenant component-registry commitment against its bundled Hebrew and Greek sources. The literal source context concerns the lawless one and manifestation; the chosen nonviolent software meanings are the inventor-directed functional reading. The executor neither identifies a person with that figure nor implements physical slaying.

R.2 Contract and expression table

The envelope policy is CE-EXPRESSION-OPERATIONS-1.255. Required fields are policy, family, expression, source_registry_ref, source_refs, interpretation, disposition, layers, target_ref, target_digest, revision, recipient_ref, expected_fruit and reassess_after_tick. Source references identify both the imagery and the humility-and-grace connection. Disposition and layer accounts are required bounded strings. expected_fruit contains endpoint, unit and criterion. A future host tick supplies the review occasion.

QUALIFY maps SLAY and a CLAIM in ACTIVE, QUALIFIED or EXPLAINED state to QUALIFIED. WITHDRAW maps those claim states to WITHDRAWN. PAUSE maps SLAY and a PROCESS in ACTIVE or REPAIR_PROPOSED state to PAUSED. CLARIFY maps SPLENDOR and an ACTIVE, QUALIFIED or EXPLAINED claim to EXPLAINED. PROPOSE_REPAIR maps SPLENDOR and an ACTIVE, PAUSED or REPAIR_PROPOSED process to REPAIR_PROPOSED. A withdrawn claim is not automatically revived by clarification. A separate explicit review and newly identified target would be needed for a replacement assertion.

The target contract requires id, kind, recipient_ref, version, content, status and source_refs. Admission is host-asserted and cannot overwrite an existing target identity. After a reviewed operation, the selected prose becomes the current internal content, the target version increases by one, the transition table sets the status, and existing source references remain unchanged. The event record retains the complete predecessor. A status of EXPLAINED means that an explanation was recorded, not that it is correct or understood. PAUSED concerns an internal process record; it does not stop an external service.

R.3 How the layers affect the proposal

Word and knowledge record the expression and available support. Heart and understanding record response priorities and learned or proposed reasoning. Milk and honey record sustaining conditions and recruitment or attention. Wind and movement record the actual proposed expression. Roots record what should persist. Fruit records intended recipient consequences and the later check. Spirit is represented by the disposition across these layers. These accounts are carried in the assessed action; editing them invalidates the existing assessment. The reference runtime checks structural completeness and commitments, not unrestricted semantic truth or the moral quality of a string.

The seven profiles retain their existing questions: purpose, prerequisites, recipient need, competing accounts, counterevidence, preservation and a bounded next step. The coordinator evaluates the complete expression-and-prose proposal using predicted profile vectors and the explicit covenant. It preserves profile floors, decline limits, confidence thresholds, full weighted penalties for predicted losses, positive participation and the separate inquiry-priority record. The framework does not assign one family exclusively to one profile.

R.4 Selection and execution algorithm

First admit an identified internal target and a recipient-bound observation snapshot. Validate one expression action per candidate against the source registry, target state and review tick. Obtain assessments of the entire candidate and run the existing covenant coordinator. Authority, preservation, source_scope, dignity, agency and support must all be required host checks. They remain host assertions; a model score cannot silently replace them. A failed check blocks and an unresolved check holds selection.

execute_expression accepts an event identity, a selected cycle identity, the expected current snapshot digest and current host checks. It verifies that the cycle is selected and not already applied, compares snapshot commitments, recomputes the guarded result and requires the same committed selection, then revalidates the target’s prechange digest. Only then does it construct a successor and commit the receipt and checkpoint atomically. This execution path has no arbitrary handler import, external callback or network action.

An exact execution retry returns the retained receipt; a changed request under the same event identity fails. A different event cannot execute an already applied cycle again. Reopening the SQLite store verifies its code/covenant manifest, event chain and checkpoint, then restores the latest target and pending-cycle states. The implementation is a trusted-host, single-writer reference. Its hashes detect inconsistency against the retained commitment; they are not signatures, proof of authorship, trusted timestamps or protection against replacement of the entire trusted environment.

R.5 State transitions and outcome discipline

Selection records AWAITING_OBSERVATION, inherited from the covenant lifecycle; an expression cycle cannot accept an outcome until its internal execution is recorded. Successful execution changes this to INTERNAL_APPLIED_AWAITING_OBSERVATION. A valid later host observation changes it to OBSERVED_REQUIRES_REASSESSMENT. A held proposal cannot execute. due_for_review(tick) returns overdue pending applied cycle identities without running a scheduler or changing state.

The outcome must match the expression’s endpoint and unit, refer to an admitted WORLD_EVENT distinct from preselection references, and have a tick strictly greater than the calculation’s observation ticks. Existing field matching and duplicate-credit checks remain in force. Adverse and confounded observations remain recorded. The baseline stays unchanged and causal attribution remains UNRESOLVED. The receipt’s INTERNAL_RECORD_TRANSITION is software evidence; it does not corroborate the claim’s semantics or certify a beneficial effect on its recipient.

R.6 Reproducible example and acceptance evidence

expression_demo.py starts with an authored overconfident number-study claim, proposes QUALIFY under SLAY, obtains a seven-profile selection and applies the internal transition. It then creates a fresh, target-bound CLARIFY proposal under SPLENDOR and applies that transition after another assessment. An authored host follow-up event is recorded separately. The example verifies predecessor retention, two version increments, exact retry behavior, all layer accounts, pending review, restart and the absence of external execution or automatic causal promotion.

Release verification comprises 461 unit tests, 16 original invariant checks, 46 authored Astral scenario checks, seven weighted-coordination comparisons, 11 covenant-lifecycle checks and 16 expression-lifecycle checks. The 38 new unit tests include every registered transition, a blocking score for each profile, changed context and target, permission revocation, unsupported-output review, registry tampering, person-target rejection, duplicate execution and observation rejection, future review and adverse-outcome retention. These are development tests and authored demonstrations. They establish the stated reference behavior within their coverage, not live Gemma or Unity performance, production concurrency, complete semantic checking or comparative research productivity.

R.7 Technical value and claim review

The concrete improvement is a reviewable connection from a layered source interpretation to a selected expression and an actual retained internal state change. A reader can inspect why a change was eligible, what was changed, what remained preserved, and which later observation still needs evaluation. Candidate product value lies in reducing unsupported certainty and making corrections auditable; such reductions and user willingness to pay need measured comparisons. No numerical business or patent valuation is inferred from the test count.

Concepts 103–106 describe source- and target-bound expression envelopes, registered corrective and clarifying transitions, execution revalidation, atomic retained history and observation separation. Counsel should compare the arrangement with existing provenance, event-sourced state machines, constrained decision systems and multi-agent review. The names and biblical inspiration do not by themselves establish novelty or nonobviousness. This is a technical revision for counsel, not an assertion that added limitations inherit an earlier filing date.
