ChristEye Physical Learning Device Design

Programmable living networks with growth support and capability replication

CE-DEV-001 • Concept design 0.3 • 13 November 2026 Brad Gearon • We Develop Software LLC

Design decision

Build a benchtop hybrid instrument: a conventional computer controls a living neuronal–astrocytic network through a commercial high-density microelectrode array. The first release uses one observable culture cartridge; a later two-cartridge release supports teaching and independent recipient testing. Preserve an expansion path for new cells and physical inter-module connections.

The living network is the learning substrate. Silicon supplies precise timing, signal conversion, the task environment, records and operating control. We do not require neurons to emulate a CPU instruction set. The preferred route is an engineering judgment based on interface feasibility and the project’s adaptive-learning goal; superior whole-device energy efficiency has not been demonstrated.

Physical completeness is a requirement

The device must actually contain the required cells and the structures they develop. Its production plan covers cell sourcing and expansion, in-cartridge maturation, axon and dendrite growth, synapse and spine formation, astrocytic integration, nourishment and replacement. A training program cannot substitute for this material structure. Each required item has an identity and function check before a capability claim.

This companion develops the physical implementation of ChristEye Knowledge Operations and Raising Design v1.2. It adopts Brad’s latest clarification of Understanding and astrocytic Knowledge links, stated on the next page. The earlier specification remains the source for task definitions and evidence boundaries. [S1]

Version 0.3 incorporates the expanded 132-row source and adds an execution plan: a context-routing demonstration, work packages, resource-gated requests and source audit. Sections 24–27 define what to build first, who owns each part and what evidence permits the next stage. [S19, S22]

1 Understanding and the starting template

For this device, Understanding is the integrated learned organization of the living network, including its engram-related state and usable response capacities. Heart names its response-forming expression: the actions reveal aspects of Understanding, but a single action does not reveal the complete underlying state. The network includes neurons and astrocytes rather than assigning all meaningful computation to one cell type.

Open development with measurable constraints

The baby analogy becomes a developmental requirement: begin with a limited repertoire, allow varied experience and new strategies, preserve acquired capability, and expand tasks when readiness is demonstrated. Do not permanently wire one neuron or electrode to one concept. Keep the physical interface stable while representations are allowed to change.

“Whatever it wants” is an aspiration toward flexible development. The system has finite sensory access, cell types, resources and learning capacity. Choosing among available experiences can be measured without claiming subjective desires. Anxiety and anger are ChristEye interpretations of proposed response patterns, not established feelings of a culture. Define any corresponding test through observable urgency, persistence, avoidance or competing responses.

Mouse studies implicate learning-associated astrocytic ensembles in recall and later stabilization. They support an integrated-neural-system hypothesis; they do not establish that all Knowledge resides in astrocytes or that this culture will reproduce the studied whole-brain functions. [S6, S7]

What prior demonstrations support

DishBrain coupled cultured neurons to a simulated environment through recording, stimulation and feedback. That supports the feasibility of a closed-loop interface and a restricted learning experiment. It does not establish a general-purpose biological CPU or unrestricted developmental intelligence. [S2]

Brainoware used an organoid as an adaptive reservoir for tasks including speech recognition and nonlinear prediction. This is a useful comparison architecture. The living contribution and the digital readout contribution must be reported separately. [S3]

Efficiency decision

For the initial small symbolic task, a silicon rule engine is the practical exact-computation choice. Whether living adaptation provides a useful advantage on unfamiliar or changing tasks is an experimental question. Compare quality, adaptation data, latency, reliability, maintenance and total energy, rather than equating low cellular energy use with an efficient complete machine.

The proposed hybrid preserves both paths: the host remains an ordinary programmable computer, while the culture develops task-related responses. No claim of general consciousness, personhood or human-level learning is needed to judge success.

3 Physical system architecture

Concept schematic. Solid arrows carry task information; dashed support arrows regulate operating conditions. The evaluator’s protected test targets are not inputs to the living network or the decoder.

Partition the device

The dry zone contains the host, acquisition hub, synchronized storage and independent support controller. The controlled culture zone contains the MEA headstage and removable culture chamber. The fluid-service zone contains identified feed and waste containers, sampling access and sensors. Provide spill containment, cable strain relief and service access without disturbing the culture.

Begin with a commercial incubator-compatible MEA and its supported chamber. A custom cartridge is a later mechanical integration task, because the cell-contact surface, electrical interface, optical path and fluid path must work together. A single device can establish interface feasibility; biological claims require independent preparations, not repeated trials treated as independent devices.

Reference mechanical envelope

The current MaxOne manufacturer page lists a 150 × 95 × 25 mm recording unit, a 185 × 280 × 90 mm hub and a 3.85 × 2.10 mm active sensor area. It distinguishes 26,400 electrode sites from 1,020 recording channels, with 20 kHz sampling per channel. These are vendor reference specifications, not final ChristEye chassis dimensions or a count of independently addressable neurons. [S5]

The final enclosure must be derived from the selected chamber, connector bend radii, microscope working distance and service clearances. Its drawing package needs a mounting datum, cartridge retention, wet/dry boundary, cable and tubing routes, optical aperture and replaceable-part access. Dimensions cannot be frozen before those interfaces are selected.

Do not assume any transparent polymer is chemically neutral. PDMS can absorb small molecules; material choice can alter the apparent exposure or the composition of a collected sample. Qualification must use the intended medium and sampling analytes. [S15]

Production strategy

For release 1, source an established neuron–astrocyte model and mature it on a supported MEA. Record source bank, donor/line where applicable, lot, developmental stage, composition and release criteria. A cell-production partner owns the qualified expansion and differentiation process. The device owns the receiving, nursing, observation and learning interfaces. This division includes growth in the plan while avoiding an unvalidated all-in-one cell factory.

Spine formation and remodeling involve actin-regulating machinery studied experimentally, but copying a spine’s visible shape is not demonstrated copying of the relation a circuit learned. Structural features and retained function require separate acceptance tests. [S13]

Production records also assign each proposed Star its observed neuronal and astrocytic participants, source lineage and interface. These are measured assemblies; a software Star ID does not manufacture a biological boundary.

Bardy and colleagues demonstrated that medium composition affects neuronal electrical and synaptic function as well as survival. Their supporting information has a later correction, reinforcing the need to use a current controlled formulation. This document specifies material categories and acceptance records, not a culture recipe. [S9, S10]

Astrocyte–neuron lactate transport and activity-linked ATP production provide component-level metabolic precedents. Their roles do not by themselves identify the content of a learned relationship. [S11, S12, S17]

7 Nursing circulation and milking

Two-cartridge concept. Each culture has its own identified feed and collection path. Shared environmental control is possible; shared liquid is excluded from the initial A-to-B teaching experiment.

Nursing responsibilities

The support subsystem maintains the qualified temperature, gas exchange, medium level, chemical state and exchange schedule. It observes temperature at the culture boundary, fluid delivery, reservoir/waste status and relevant chemical measurements. pH, osmolality and selected metabolite measurements may combine installed sensors with scheduled sampling; an estimate must be labeled as such.

Use the selected platform’s supported exchange method for release 1. Continuous perfusion is a later qualified option: it can change local signaling, remove secreted factors and introduce bubbles, shear or gradients. The public MaxOne perfusion accessory is described for acute recording; long-term culture perfusion compatibility must therefore be established separately. [S5]

What milking means in this device

There are two distinct interpretations. Cells provide endogenous support through metabolism and signaling; that is part of nursing. A sampling outlet can also collect a limited, recorded fraction of conditioned medium for measurement of metabolites, proteins or other candidate cargo. Collected output is an analytical sample, not a demonstrated extract of Understanding.

Record sampled volume, replacement, timing, parent cartridge, storage/assay identity and disturbance to the culture. Collection cannot silently become a cross-feed into B. A future conditioned-medium transfer study needs its own controls for support, stress, contamination, source attribution and content-specific recipient behavior. Sample recovery and molecular transport alone do not prove memory transfer. [S18]

Nourishment remains available independently of task success. Outcome feedback changes the information presented to the learner; it does not withhold basic support as a reward or punishment.

8 Programming the Winds

The device is programmable through an interface contract. A program defines the environment, input meanings, permitted stimulation profiles, output interpretation, feedback rules and evaluation partitions. The host executes ordinary code. The culture is trained through experiences; it is not assumed to execute that code as CPU instructions.

Software and timing

Use the host for task orchestration and durable records. Place time-critical I/O in the vendor interface or a verified real-time process; an independent controller handles operating limits. A web UI may submit programs and display status, but it must not supply precise stimulus timing. Measure clock offsets, end-to-end latency, jitter and dropped events against the task’s locked deadlines.

Cortical Labs publishes a programmable recording/stimulation API and a simulator intended for interface development. That is evidence that a code-controlled biological interface is practical, not evidence that its simulator reproduces biological learning. Exact compatibility with the proposed cell composition and custom fluidics needs vendor confirmation. [S4, S16]

Keep two explicit learning modes: biological adaptation with a fixed evaluation readout, and a biological-reservoir mode with a separately trained digital readout. Their results must not be pooled as the same claim.

9 Raising the network through experience

The first task retains the master specification’s sequence/context test. Under C0, AB calls for X and BA for Y; C1 reverses those responses. AA and BB call for HOLD. Missing context or an unfamiliar token yields interface status UNRESOLVED. Repeated symbols prevent a final-symbol shortcut. These are grounded virtual events and actions, not evidence of natural-language understanding. [S1]

Strengthening template Understanding

Brad’s proposal is that the initial organization has limited response options and can develop more effective ones through trained Winds. Test that claim by defining an initial competing-response task and measuring whether later experience produces a more context-sensitive action, better recovery and retained capability. Avoid treating stronger activity, reduced movement or fewer responses as sufficient improvement.

If anxiety and anger name modeled urgent or persistent responses, specify their behavioral indicators and alternatives before testing. A constructive alternative should meet the declared need under the task rules. Neither a signal’s intensity nor apparent quietness proves the network’s emotional state or the quality of its Understanding.

Keep learning, consolidation/quiet observation and evaluation as distinguishable phases. Do not maximize stimulation or growth continuously. Developmental opportunity means varied information and room for adaptation within a maintained operating envelope.

First physical replication experiment

Build A and B from documented compatible sources in separate culture cartridges. Establish A’s task capability and B’s baseline independently. During a declared teaching phase, allow a logged electronic path from A-derived activity or actions to B’s input. That is a physical experiment using living modules, with a programmable bridge that can be disconnected unambiguously.

For the B-only test, remove A-derived live signals, cached outputs, adaptive shared decoder state and teaching feedback. B still receives the ordinary task observations. Score B from its own recorded activity with its frozen readout. Separately retest A to detect loss caused by teaching or integration.

Compare B-only training, trained A, untrained A, shuffled/yoked A signals and matched conventional teaching. Hold exposure, support and decoder capacity comparable. A useful result establishes independent acquisition within the tested scope; it does not establish microscopic engram identity.

Why start with an electronic bridge

It provides controllable access, direction, delay and separation while keeping both living networks real. A permanent axon bridge cannot be disabled without considering tissue disturbance. Physical axon-connected organoid research supplies a communication and plasticity precedent; independent transfer of the specified capability remains a separate test. [S8]

11 Growing new components and integrating them

Growth is an explicit device workstream. It begins with a source/production compartment and a maturation cartridge, then progresses to a characterized recipient network. The initial chassis does not mix uncontrolled progenitor proliferation into a trained core. This keeps development observable and allows incompatible material to be excluded before integration.

Later physical joining module

A future cartridge may place neuronal populations in separated chambers with a designated axonal connection region and independent observation access. The mechanical design must distinguish an axon-permissive path from chemical diffusion, pressure-driven exchange and electrical cross-talk. Those routes require separate measurements; chamber separation by itself does not prove isolation.

Astrocytic integration is part of the living network design. Measure whether added astrocytes support useful relationships beyond changes in survival, general activity or resource supply. If the Knowledge-link hypothesis predicts selective retrieval, compare the intended relation against matched unrelated relations. A general health benefit remains valuable but supports a narrower conclusion. [S6, S7]

A replaced culture cartridge carries a new biological state. Restoring software, the curriculum and the decoder does not restore the old Understanding. Preserve history and source identity, then demonstrate what the new network acquires or retains.

Diagnose before changing the Roots

When an output fails, preserve the input, delivered signal, raw response, support state and decision history. Investigate absent knowledge, inaccessible retained state, interface failure, insufficient resources, changed task context and actual damage. Use permitted discriminating observations; return UNRESOLVED when several causes remain compatible with the evidence.

Nyxari’s requirement preserves the history of removals and alternatives, including uncertainty about unobserved events. Maera’s requirement records resource costs and reserve limits. Support restoration, route repair, retraining and growth are different interventions. A recovering score does not automatically establish restoration of the original biological mechanism. [S1]

The hidden-fault software harness must first demonstrate that the controller can distinguish corrupted learned parameters, blocked access and temporary execution-budget loss. Translate only those distinctions with observable biological counterparts into experiments. It is acceptable to leave a biological cause unresolved rather than infer it from the software analogy.

The diagnostic decision table in section 18 and the separate Mesh records in section 20 distinguish structural, functional and intervention-supported findings. Version 0.3 adds context-confusion and help-request tests in section 24. Preserved anatomy, synchrony or recovery after rest alone cannot decide which mechanism failed.

Independent control

The support controller enforces limits independently of the task program. Its limit set includes allowed electrical profiles, cumulative exposure, thermal/fluid bounds, sensor freshness and recovery rules. These values are platform-specific release items. A speculative learner must not invent them or optimize around them.

For electronics commissioning, verify timing, data loss, artifact recovery and fault suppression using the vendor-supported test setup before a living cartridge is exposed. Verify fluid delivery and containment in a cell-free assembled path. The system records these engineering checks separately from biological learning results.

The proposed instrument is for controlled in-vitro research. Cell sourcing, handling and any advanced developmental model require the responsible facility’s established oversight. Increasing developmental complexity is a separate decision; it is not an automatic reward for higher task performance.

14 Efficiency and engineering budgets

Efficiency is evaluated at matched useful performance. Report task accuracy, calibrated abstention, adaptation exposure, latency, retention, availability and total resource use. A low-power culture with unreliable outputs is not an efficient replacement for a reliable smaller silicon system.

Illustrative data budget

Using the reference platform’s 1,020 channels at 20 kHz and an assumed 16-bit stored sample gives 40.8 MB/s, or 146.9 GB per hour before overhead. The 16-bit storage assumption is illustrative, not a verified vendor file format. Two simultaneously recorded modules double this stream. Confirm actual packing, retained channels and storage duty cycle before procurement. [S5]

Decision rule for a computing advantage

Compare a rule engine on exact known tasks, a compact trained digital recurrent model on adaptive tasks, and the living hybrid at the same success and latency targets. Use whole-run watt-hours divided by accepted correct decisions as one service metric, while reporting abstentions and errors separately. Do not omit energy spent keeping an idle culture alive or use a large unrelated GPU workload as the sole comparator.

No energy advantage is assumed in this design. If the living network provides a useful adaptation or mechanistic-research capability at greater cost, report that value honestly. Vendor low-energy claims and brain-level estimates cannot replace matched measurements of this device.

Two practical supplier routes

The reference research route is a commercial HD-MEA with a qualified neuron–astrocyte culture, because it gives the project control over cell composition and observation. MaxOne is a concrete specification reference, not an assertion that every proposed custom feature is supported. Confirm long-term support, optical access, simultaneous recording/stimulation and custom control requirements before selecting a package. [S5]

An integrated biological-computing platform such as CL1 offers another route for early task/interface work. Its published materials describe integrated life support and programmable interaction. Confirm cell composition, astrocyte observability, source control, raw-data access, maintenance and supported joining experiments rather than assuming equivalence to this design. [S4, S16]

Do not order a custom chip, build a cell-production facility or specify a final chassis before the selected platform and culture pair pass interface and support qualification. The next purchasable result is a vendor-confirmed subsystem list with current quotes and integration drawings.

Open release items

The selected cell source, lineage composition, cell density, maturation criteria, medium/supplements, exchange method, chamber dimensions, stimulation envelope, sensor tolerances and optical assays remain platform-qualified parameters. The biological preparation is not specified by the ChristEye names alone. Each parameter needs an owner, evidence source, qualification result and locked version before fabrication or live operation depends on it.

The manufacturing package must contain the supplier bill of materials, interface/control drawings, serviceable cartridge design, electrical/grounding plan, fluid-path drawing, firmware build, calibration records and acceptance report. This concept establishes what those deliverables must accomplish; it is not a claim that the drawings or biological production process have already been completed.

Provenance and continuity

This document incorporates Brad’s requests for open development through trained Winds, integrated Understanding expressed by Heart actions, astrocytic Knowledge links and physical replication/growth of all required parts. Those are design requirements or hypotheses. DishBrain, Brainoware and the cited molecular/cellular studies supply scoped precedents. The six Stars’ earlier contributions remain represented through integrity, uncertainty, routing, causal care, history and resource accounting. [S1]

The first engineering milestone is a supported, observable living cartridge with a verified programmable interface. The first learning milestone is a retained capability beyond controls. The first replication milestone is independent recipient capability. None has been claimed as completed here.

Primary work on fluorescent calcium sensors provides a precedent for observing neural activity with optical measurements. Proposed engineering models

These equations govern optional task and growth actions; they are not established equations of biological Understanding. Basic life support remains independently maintained, regardless of task scores or deadlines. Parameters and uncertainty must be estimated for the selected task.

Feasible(a) requires, for every resource j: r_j - committed_j - cost_j(a) >= reserve_j and delivered_time(a) <= deadline and all qualified operating limits hold.

Each resource is evaluated in its own units: fluid volume, storage capacity, processing time or another measured budget. Do not add incompatible quantities or treat an inferred “life force” as a calibrated resource. Acquisition of a missing resource is itself an action subject to the same limits.

a* = argmax over feasible a of E[task utility(a) | evidence] VOI(q) = E[best feasible utility after q] - best utility now - cost(q) - expected cost of waiting for q

Search when the estimated value of information, VOI, is positive and the deadline permits it. Otherwise choose an admissible action, HOLD or UNRESOLVED.
