CHRISTEYE

KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING COMPUTER

Living Neural Substrate Research Design Specification

Version 1.0 · 13 November 2026 · Incorporating Physical Learning Device, Sylph, and Observational Network Architecture

1. Document Purpose and Scientific Scope

This specification defines a staged, nonclinical research design for a biohybrid knowledge and understanding system. It integrates the Physical Learning Device concept (CE-DEV-001), the Sylph cancer-surveillance architecture, the ChristEye Living Table functional vocabulary, and the observational-network design developed in prior technical discussion.

The design is hypothesis-generating and experimental. No cells, substrates, or networks described here are for swallowing, injection, implantation, or use outside approved laboratory biosafety and institutional oversight. The document does not assert demonstrated knowledge extraction, understanding transfer, therapeutic benefit, or clinical safety.

Framework terms (Soul, Spirit, Star, Tree, Marrow, Lotus, Mesh, Soil, Milk, Honey, Roots, Winds, Heart, Astroengram) are used as functional organizing vocabulary. They map to measurable biological and engineering variables; they do not replace identification, quantification, or evidence requirements.

Primary research question: Does a contained, closed-loop trained living neuronal–astrocytic network (or modular network of networks) improve detection, classification, retention, and controlled response to predefined sensor patterns beyond an ordinary software controller, without increasing nonspecific activity or harming the culture?

2. Overall System Architecture

The system is a hybrid living-silicon instrument. A conventional computer supplies the task environment, precise timing, signal conversion, logging, and independent operating control. The living network is the adaptive learning substrate. The preferred first implementation is a single observable culture cartridge on a commercial high-density microelectrode array (HD-MEA). A later dual-cartridge configuration supports teaching and independent recipient testing. Physical inter-module axon-permissive connections are an expansion path, not a first-build requirement.

Physical completeness is required. The device must contain the cells and the structures they develop (neurons, astrocytes, axons, dendrites, spines, synapses, receptors, and scaffolds). A training program cannot substitute for this material structure. Each required item has an identity and function check before a capability claim.

2.1 Modular Partition

Modules are independently evaluable so that learning, joining, and contribution can be measured separately.

ModuleRole, constraints, and first-build status
Observational / sensor networkSeparate cartridge or fluidically restricted compartment. Closed-loop trained on encoded normal / injury / malignant-pattern signals. Produces clear decision output. Astrocyte participation monitored. Trained and evaluated independently before any joining.
Core understanding / main meshPrimary living learner on HD-MEA (planar co-culture or organoid). Receives observational output as one input. Trained for task performance, retention, and transfer. First-build focus.
Support / monitoring communitiesAdditional neuronal and astrocytic populations. Stabilize, route, or report. Joined first electronically, later by controlled axon-permissive paths. Independently evaluated. Expansion path.
Host computerTask oracle, encoding, frozen evaluation readout, independent limit controller (Heart), logging, and resource gating. Does not replace the living substrate.

2.2 Mechanical Stack and Interfaces (Reference)

Culture boundary: vendor-supported chamber/lid on MEA substrate with controlled gas exchange. Cell-contact layer: qualified adhesion/ECM interface. Living layer: observable neuronal–astrocytic co-culture. Recording interface: commercial HD-MEA headstage and hub. Wetted service path: qualified reservoirs and tubing. Carrier and enclosure: rigid serviceable carrier with wet/dry separation and microscope access.

Reference platform (MaxOne-class): 26,400 electrode sites, 1,020 recording channels, 20 kHz sampling. These are vendor specifications, not final ChristEye chassis dimensions or a count of independently addressable neurons. Final enclosure derives from selected chamber, connector geometry, and service access.

3. Cell Lineages and Generation (Marrow)

All lineages start from a qualified iPSC bank with documented identity, karyotype, and genomic stability. Differentiation uses established dual-SMAD inhibition to neural progenitors, followed by regional patterning and neurotrophic support. “Crown and Lotus machinery” and “embryo creation” map exclusively to controlled embryoid-body, neural-organoid, or patterned-progenitor generation under standard laboratory protocols. Full viable embryos are neither required nor proposed.

3.1 Observational Network Lineage

Dual-SMAD inhibition to neural progenitors / neuroepithelium.

Optional cortical or mixed regionalization.

Differentiation to glutamatergic and GABAergic neurons plus astrocytes, or cortical organoids plated on HD-MEA.

Precedent: DishBrain closed-loop learning (Kagan et al., 2022); organoid cartpole learning (Robbins et al., Cell Reports 2026).

The observational network is trained separately so that its response is driven by detection of changes in the observed (encoded) system. Astrocyte participation is monitored; selective contribution of learning-associated astrocyte ensembles is tested with delayed, controlled assays rather than assumed.

3.2 Core Mesh Lineage

Neural progenitors patterned with retinoic acid + SHH pathway agonist for ventral spinal identity (OLIG2 → ISL1/ChAT).

Co-culture with spinal-patterned astrocytes (Taga et al., 2019 precedent for accelerated maturation on MEA).

Optional interneuron populations for diversity (“Spirits of different types”).

Spinal patterning is preferred for the first build because of documented co-culture maturation benefits and relevance to the Tree-of-Knowledge / spine-related framing in the source materials.

3.3 Support and Monitoring Lineages

Additional astrocyte lots (spinal or cortical) and selected interneurons with optical reporters.

Identity, viability, morphology, and electrophysiological release criteria before any joining.

Support communities are raised under the same progenitor bank and patterning logic but matured separately. Their “winds” (signal output) are shaped by outcome-contingent feedback tied to measured contribution to the main mesh.

4. Soil, Milk, and Honey Conditions

4.1 Soil (Receiving Context)

Qualified extracellular matrix (PDL/PLO + laminin ± fibronectin or Matrigel) on the electrode surface. BrainPhys-class medium + SM1/N2 + BDNF/GDNF (typically 20 ng/mL range, platform-qualified) + ascorbic acid. Continuous monitoring of osmolality, pH, glucose/lactate, and ion balance. Medium exchange schedule is locked after qualification. Excessive ECM coverage must not mask electrode signals.

4.2 Milk (Support / Raising Capacity)

Qualified medium, trophic factors, and metabolic support that maintain viability, maturation, and the capacity for activity. Nourishment is never withheld as reward or punishment. Basic life support is enforced by the independent controller regardless of task score. Milk raises the conditions under which Spirits (neurons) and astrocytic participation can develop and operate.

4.3 Honey (Selection / Attraction / Stabilization)

Structured closed-loop stimulation and outcome-contingent feedback that preferentially stabilize useful response patterns. For the observational network this is detection-contingent feedback. For support networks it is feedback tied to measured contribution. Honey attracts and stabilizes the external (or inter-module) system responses that the network builds. No bulk chemical is treated as automatic Honey or as a memory code.

5. Training Processes

5.1 Observational Network Training (DishBrain-style)

The observational network is trained separately so that astroengrams (learning-associated astrocyte ensembles) and neuronal patterns allow it to respond when it detects changes in the observed system. Reinforcement and the developed learning process are used to root understanding so that the network reacts to sensor input before conditions degrade.

Encode sensor patterns (normal / injury / malignant-associated feature vectors) as validated electrical stimulation profiles (rate and place coding, artifact-masked, within locked limits).

Define output regions decoded into a decision (alert, hold, or class).

Deliver structured feedback: predictable low-cost stimuli for correct early detection; higher-cost or less predictable stimuli for misses or false alarms.

Include rest / consolidation periods. Responses during learning or rest phases are logged for retention and recall analysis.

Freeze the evaluation readout. Score on held-out patterns against untrained, noncontingent-feedback, and pure-software baselines.

Success requires incremental classification, calibration, stability, and retention. A negative result is informative. Astrocyte Ca or reporter signals are correlated with task-related ensembles; selective contribution requires delayed, controlled tests. Cells can be trained as sensors for retrieval operations and accuracy of pulling patterns through astrocyte messaging.

5.2 Core Mesh Training

Closed-loop embodiment in task environments (DishBrain-style or organoid cartpole-style). Feedback contingent on performance. Retention and transfer tested after delay with frozen readout. The main mesh receives the observational network’s decision as one input among others after the observational network has passed its independent gates.

5.3 Sensor Training for Retrieval and Monitoring

Dedicated monitoring cells (neuronal and marrow-related) are trained to analyze and support the mesh. They act as sensors for retrieval operations and the accuracy of pattern recall via astrocyte messaging. Their output is used during learning or rest periods to improve retention and recall efficiency. Training uses the same closed-loop principles but with feedback contingent on measured contribution to mesh stability and accuracy rather than primary task performance.

6. Joining Processes

6.1 Stage 1 — Electronic Bridge (Preferred First)

Logged, directionally controllable, delay-capable pathway from observational-network output to main-mesh input electrodes. Disconnectable without tissue disturbance. During teaching the bridge is active; for isolation tests it is removed, cached outputs cleared, and readout frozen. Compare against yoked/shuffled signals and matched software teaching. This is the first method of attachment by the Milk and Honey process.

6.2 Stage 2 — Physical Axon-Permissive Joining

Microfluidic or PDMS microchannel geometries, or axon-bundle connections between organoids. Route-specific measurements distinguish axonal communication from chemical coupling, common drive, or leakage. Independent observation access for each compartment. This stage is later and separately gated.

6.3 Support / Child Networks

Generated from matched progenitor bank under the same patterning, matured separately. “Crown decision in embryo creation” maps to the choice of differentiation factors and plating geometry. Support networks receive feedback contingent on measured contribution to the main mesh so their activity patterns become useful. They remain completely attached as child neuron networks by the host’s decision at the level of generation and routing, but evaluation stays independent.

7. Control Method for Support Networks (Seduction of Winds)

The host maintains a NeedRequest / resource-gate record (missing relation, observations, confidence, deadline, allowed recipients). Support networks receive encoded requests or state summaries and produce candidate responses. A Lotus-style host coordinator compares predicted utility against resource reserves and protected capabilities (ACT / HOLD / UNRESOLVED). Outcome-contingent structured stimulation increases the probability of useful support patterns within locked electrical and metabolic limits. An independent Heart/safety controller can veto any action that violates operating bounds or protected old relations. All interventions are reversible electronic routing first. Structural changes require separate gates.

This method is designed to seduce the support networks to build their winds (signal flow) in service of the main network’s understanding layer, growth, and upkeep without compromising independent evaluation or safety.

8. Mesh, Roots, and Diagnostic Architecture

Mesh records are typed relationships among components (functional, statistical, intervention, and task relations). Roots are retained organization (synapses, spines, and activity patterns that survive delay and transfer tests). Diagnosis separates retention, access, capacity, context, and interface hypotheses using delayed trials, instrument checks, protected relations, and an unresolved state. Words and Wind queries carry defined meaning, provenance, recipient, and timing. Crystal and Seed separate a preserved record from a viable source and from learned capability.

9. Evidence Gates and Evaluation

Follow a staged G1–G6 structure specialized for multi-network work:

G1–G3: Source release, maturation, and interface competence for each lineage separately.

Observational training and retention must pass before joining.

Electronic teaching bridge and isolation tests.

Support-network contribution measured against fixed-schedule and non-adaptive controls.

Resource cost, old-task retention, and failure modes reported.

Optical or molecular assays only when required for the specific claim.

Efficiency is whole-device (host + acquisition + stimulation + support + storage). No energy advantage is assumed. Negative results are valuable and must be reported. Illustrative data budget (reference platform, 1,020 channels at 20 kHz, 16-bit): ~40.8 MB/s or ~146.9 GB/hour before overhead; two modules double this stream. Confirm actual packing and duty cycle before procurement.

10. Safety, Independent Stop Rules, and Non-Claims

Independent stop rules and blinded outcome measurements apply to every arm. Any unanticipated growth, toxicity, instrument malfunction, or unreviewed methodological change triggers pause and review. Neuronal signals can encourage growth in some tumors; this possibility is tested explicitly where relevant (Sylph context). No version is self-administered or tested in a person outside a formally approved program.

Reasonable research claim: The system investigates adaptive biological pattern processing with longitudinal personalization potential, neuronal and astrocytic signal handling, and regulated support modules under independent safety control.

Not established: Daily or repeated dosing growing functional networks in a person; automatic identification of all cancer-related damage; reliable tumor killing by neural cells; prevention or cure of disease; safe lifelong integration; transferable Astroengrams as molecular packets; or superiority over software on any clinical endpoint.

11. Open Parameters and Next Deliverables

Cell densities, exact ratios, stimulation envelopes, optical assays, maturation criteria, and microchannel dimensions remain platform- and culture-qualified items. They are locked before live operation depends on them.

Subsystem compatibility matrix.

Supplier-confirmed parts list (HD-MEA platform, qualified iPSC sources, medium formulation).

Source and maturation acceptance criteria.

Interface drawings for electronic bridge and optional physical joining.

Pre-registered analysis plan with held-out sets, negative controls, and missing-data rules.

Document status: Proposed research architecture. Any use of human cells requires institutional review, biosafety assessment, and applicable regulatory authorization. This specification does not assert demonstrated knowledge extraction, understanding transfer, or therapeutic benefit.
