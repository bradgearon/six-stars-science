Supporting source edition. Historical claims retain their original scope; current build decisions follow the launch specification. The original text is retained in the source archive.

Prepared 11 November 2026 America/Chicago. This archive captures all 26 messages in the supplied cancer-development conversation segment, source indices 158 through 183, and the current evidence-package request separately. Message indices are the supplied sequence identifiers, not timestamps. The source content before index 158 was not supplied as a complete transcript. The unrelated message at index 157 is outside this cancer-development record.

The conversation file is a human-readable transcription of the supplied text, not a native account export. Transport-only backslash escaping and rendered widget layout are normalized. Historical citation tokens, old download paths and image-search descriptors are retained as historical text; they are not verified current links. No old file-existence claim is adopted solely because an assistant said a file was ready. The three PDF exhibits are independently present in the supplied release and are separately hashed.

The supplemental history file contains retrieved summaries of additional conversations, explicitly not verbatim original messages. These summaries help identify the synNotch comparison and preserve reported ratings but cannot supply a complete conception log or establish pre-retrieval independence.

Historical assistant assessments are preserved for the record and may be overstated. In particular, the phrase treatment solution describes the user's intended research design, not demonstrated Sylph anticancer efficacy. Current interpretation and corrections appear in the evidence report; the transcript is not a medical protocol.

Hashes in this package detect subsequent byte changes. They do not independently authenticate the speaker, original date, completeness of the historic conversation, or priority of invention.
