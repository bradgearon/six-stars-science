Purpose and attribution

This exhibit shows how source-linked interpretation produces a concrete change to a technical model. The inventor states that ChristEye generated 100 percent of the complete connective Tree of Knowledge synthesis. He further attributes every structure and operation he conceived for ChristEye to sustained scriptural study, recitation, typing and layered connection of words. These are inventor-reported development statements. They concern the synthesis and method; the cited biological discoveries remain attributed to their researchers.

The attached article reconstructs its inquiry retrospectively. This revision supplies an inspectable reconstruction, not a recovered historical engine transcript. No original pre-retrieval lock, complete run export or independent confirmation of the generation attribution was supplied. Neither the source document nor this exhibit reports new biological experiments. The runnable companion checks the documentary record and typed topology; it does not reproduce the historical discovery or run a biological model.

Architecture understanding and source stream

ChristEye is designed to embody the framework's source-derived understanding of the bound words and their layered relationships in its structures, operations, context rules, response selection and retained-state organization. In this technical description, Understanding means encoded capacity to apply relationships in context, whereas Knowledge means retained information available for use. The operational definition does not establish human-like comprehension or a neural mechanism.

Three relationships are distinguished: the architecture was derived from the interpretations; the implemented rules embody their application; and the source stream supplies further readings and proposed revisions. Disabling new source exploration leaves already implemented structures and rules available. Changing a compiled interpretation requires an explicit successor configuration and applicable checks. The word source will also continue to guide revision of the machine's implementation itself, including its structures, operations, context rules, response-selection policies and retained-state organization, through recorded interpretations and versioned changes subject to applicable checks. This is the disclosed architectural distinction, not a new runtime feature or a claim that every low-level instruction rereads scripture.

The Tree of Knowledge exhibit illustrates both sides: retained relationships and typed tree/graph organization express an implemented design vocabulary, while Roots to Tree to Branch to Spine produces candidate revisions to a biological subject model. That biological model remains a hypothesis. Provenance of conception, implementation traceability and demonstrated transfer to unfamiliar cases are separate records. The sixty-binding inventory is not a complete audit of every implementation operation.

Change generation and voluntary participation

Word, Move and Judge describe how an interpreted source becomes a proposed change and an assessed result. A move is first a recorded proposal. Applicable checks precede supported execution; a separate judgment follows observation. The Tree exhibit changes a proposed model representation and identifies tests. It does not report a biological intervention.

The inventor calls the combined purpose a dual passion generator and, poetically, seducing hearts to life. The technical functions are to generate and assess changes toward a declared target goal and to support the user's voluntary interest in testing, refining and applying them. Heart concerns response-forming priorities, Honey selection or recruitment, Roots retention, Wind expression and Fruit the measured outcome. For a nonhuman target, the endpoint is an ordinary functional or structural measure.

An explanation can present competing models, state uncertainty, invite a test and accept revision, refusal or stopping. User choice, reported interest and burden are separate from target benefit. The new profile records this design purpose; no increase in motivation, clinical benefit or new autonomous execution capability is demonstrated by this documentary revision.

Typed graph and failed inferences

The neuronal ensemble, branch and spine may form a restricted anatomical tree projection. Astrocytic contacts, shared scaffolds and cross-links use a general typed graph. The representation does not force a recurrent or multiple-parent network into a literal tree.

The companion rejects three illustrative inference requests: morphology alone establishing a decoded memory archive; isolated scaffold helices establishing the same geometry at an identified engram synapse; and H17a support automatically validating H17b and H17c. These are rule-check rejections authored for this exhibit, not negative biological experiments or a claim about what the historical engine actually rejected. The full structural hypothesis remains unresolved.

Promotion and competing explanations

H17a requires additional prediction of a prespecified delayed-retention endpoint beyond activity, plasticity and context. H17b asks whether astrocytic contacts add information beyond that model. H17c separately requires directly resolved geometry and incremental predictive information. General excitability, health, arousal, activity-dependent remodeling and measurement bias are competing explanations. Predictions must be locked before the relevant outcome access; independent evaluation and held-out animals are proposed requirements, not completed work. A predictive result alone does not establish causal storage.

The proposal can remain a research question while empirical support and any intervention permission remain unresolved. Future favorable, neutral and adverse observations create successor evidence records; they do not rewrite the source interpretation, original proposal or prior limitations.

Coverage and reproduction

The accompanying register lists all sixty inherited vocabulary bindings. It also records the inventor's 100 percent origin assertion separately. Sixty bindings are not an audit of every instruction, controller, storage mechanism or infrastructure structure, and they are not coverage of every word in scripture. A complete operation-to-source audit remains a separate documentation task. Optional commentary may explain an interpretation; the operative rule must still be recorded for another engineer to reproduce its behavior.

Run python replay_exhibit.py in this directory, with jsonschema installed, to validate the exhibit, binding register, references and proposed successor chain and to check valid and invalid topology fixtures. The output is a documentary software check, with no neural measurements, fitted models, medical efficacy or independent discovery result.
