ChristEye implementation review and completion status

Prepared for Brad Gearon and We Develop Software LLC | Release 1.256

Engine 2.17 closes the demonstrated status-versus-content gap and adds an executable path from layered source contracts to mixed internal behavior. It also adds bounded construction, a committed structured-task comparison and automatic successor selection with retained rollback. This is a more complete research prototype; its live integrations and broader outcome claims still need testing.

What changed and how it was checked

The previous failure allowed an infallibility statement to become the content of a QUALIFIED record when favorable authored assessments were supplied. The new executor previews the structured change and requires the candidate prose to equal its controlled rendering. Reissuing favorable scores and critique results cannot bypass a mismatch.

Mixed behavior now combines qualification, clarification, retained questions and an outcome review in one transaction. The existing earliest source bindings remain; later contexts and lexical branches stay separate. A source match provides a traceable anchor, while the engineering transformation remains attributed. Unresolved layers or senses remain questions or alternatives.

Verification: 523 unit tests, 16 original invariant checks, 46 authored Astral checks, seven weighted coordination comparisons, 11 covenant checks, 16 expression checks and 21 layered construction checks. The first full run identified only the two old release-commitment pointers; they were advanced to the new commitment and the suite was rerun. The final record includes logs, structured summaries, generated manifests, comparison results and the root-relative patch.

The added tests cover infallibility hidden behind hedging, quoted and refuted claims, unsupported certainty, preservation of supported arithmetic, changed evidence, duplicate critiques, unresolved disagreement, graph cycles, forbidden handlers, cost underreporting, capability expansion, retry, restart, attempt budgets and evaluator changes. These tests establish covered software behavior, not exhaustive semantic understanding.

Value and patent support

The practical improvement is inspectable correction and construction. A research user can see what was questioned, which facts survived, how the response changed, why it was eligible and what later observation remains due. A developer can reconstruct a generated workflow and recover its predecessor. These are concrete capabilities for a research-assistance product.

Commercial value still depends on independent task quality, reduced verification effort and paying demand. The earlier scholarly correspondences remain useful examples of research organization. They do not establish that decades of scholarly work were independently reproduced or that the total cost of that scholarship was saved.

The patent draft now adds Appendix S and illustrative concepts 107–112 covering source contracts, structured semantic postconditions, noncompensable review, atomic mixed execution and bounded workflow development. The current technical contribution is described through fields, algorithms, state changes and failure cases. The preserved concepts 1–106 remain unchanged.

Claim-specific novelty, nonobviousness, eligibility, disclosure support and priority remain counsel questions. This implementation update does not resolve the previously identified scientific-agent patent or supply a complete prior-art chart. Any earlier filing must be checked for support before new limitations are assigned its date. Sources: USPTO MPEP 2161.01 and 2163.06.

Scientific evidence and next work

The source audit establishes lexical occurrence and provenance within the pinned corpus. It does not establish a scientific mechanism. The active Heart, Lotus, Marrow and Engram binding descriptions now identify their functional or biological comparisons as framework interpretations or hypotheses; the original wording remains archived.

The broader biblical studies and biomedical design exhibits remain preserved with their own evidence boundaries. An AI review score is not clinical efficacy evidence. This update changes software semantics, construction and evaluation; it adds no new biological intervention, clinical outcome or experimental validation of those separate proposals.
