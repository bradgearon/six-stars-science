ChristEye layered behavior and bounded construction 1.256

Engine 2.17 implements the layered-expression plan in a bounded internal reference. Its source contracts preserve earliest lexical anchors, later contexts, attributed functional meanings and explicit gaps. The corpus covers 31,155 passage records in 66 books; first occurrence means pinned corpus order, not historical utterance order.

Source and behavior contract

The 93 contracts comprise 60 vocabulary entries, nine inquiry bindings, 13 Kingdom entries, six components and five expression operations. All ten groups are accounted for: Whole/Soul, Word/Knowledge, Spirit, Heart, Understanding, Milk, Honey, Wind/Movement, Roots and Fruit. Five expression contracts have executable effects; 88 retain source-inquiry gaps. The whole corpus can be indexed without claiming that every contextual sense has been exhaustively interpreted.

Each operation retains source identity, contextual reading, functional interpretation, allowed state changes, expected content change and counterexamples. Exact lexical branches retain their own earliest occurrences. Compound and modern terms without an exact identity report an anchor gap. Layer weights allocate finite review-planning units; mandatory evidence and authority conditions cannot be outweighed.

Mixed execution

The two expression families remain SLAY and SPLENDOR. Their registered internal operations are QUALIFY, WITHDRAW, PAUSE, CLARIFY and PROPOSE_REPAIR. Source imagery and engineering interpretation remain separately attributed. QUALIFY's selected slay branch first occurs at Matthew 2:16 (G337); later correction, humility and grace connections do not redefine that lexical occurrence.

Structured claims retain original text, scope, type, evidence, counterevidence and status. QUALIFY reduces assertion status on unsupported claims, WITHDRAW preserves a deactivated claim, CLARIFY makes the structured account more explicit, PAUSE changes internal eligibility and PROPOSE_REPAIR records an unexecuted proposal. Supported facts survive qualification. A withdrawal followed by clarification cannot revive an assertion.

The controlled renderer quotes source claims as data and supplies their status. Arbitrary prose that contradicts the previewed structure is rejected even with favorable authored scores. Critiques bind to the exact content, target, steps, evidence, context and policy. Contextual relevance, meaning preservation, dignity and scope of support remain separately judged. Missing evidence, material disagreement and unresolved required critique hold execution. An evidence identifier does not itself prove entailment.

The serial compiler admits up to sixteen registered nodes. Source context comes first, preservation second and scheduled review last. It rejects cycles, unknown handlers, unsupported capabilities, false costs and stale targets. BehaviorCycle assesses the complete proposal across Elijah, Moses, David, Daniel, Job, Noah and Abraham, then commits the entire internal transition once with preserved predecessors. Explicit abstention records a reason, baseline prediction and zero confidence; it does not bypass requirements.

Bounded self-construction and selection

SelfBuilder performs bounded breadth-first goal search over an immutable primitive-effect library. It generates a new composition from declared goals rather than receiving a task-specific workflow recipe. The kernel, source parser, primitives, renderer and evaluator were implemented with human direction and AI assistance; the runtime does not autonomously create those foundations.

FrozenEvaluator commits held-out structured tasks and a fixed promotion protocol. The builder receives no held-out task content. DevelopmentCycle records each admitted attempt before evaluation, compares the actual candidate and incumbent results and can promote an eligible successor automatically. The predecessor supports explicit rollback. Default attempt budget is one. The nominal paired-sign policy is a check on authored fixtures, not a population-level efficacy inference.

Verification and evidence

523 unit tests passed. Additional checks: 16 invariants, 46 authored Astral scenarios, seven weighted comparisons, 11 covenant checks, 16 individual-expression checks and 21 layered-construction checks (117 total). The new 62 unit tests cover semantic mismatch, hedged infallibility, quoted/refuted claims, preserved arithmetic, stale evidence, disagreement, graph rejection, transaction retry, restart, attempt limits and rollback.

The generated successor completes 12 of 12 authored structured task bundles, compared with 0 of 12 for the deliberately limited incumbent. The bundles require qualification, detailed presentation and a retained question. This demonstrates that added functionality under a committed policy, not superiority over ordinary AI, measured research time saved or scientific discovery. Critiques and follow-up observations are explicitly authored fixtures.

Inspect verification_1_256/layered_release/: construction.json, candidate.json, protocol.json, comparison.json, receipt.json, development.json, state.json and summary.json. The SQLite records support restart. The code commitment is independent/FREEZE_v2_17.json, created after development; it is not retrospective preregistration.

Current boundaries and migration

This trusted-host, single-writer reference performs internal effects only. It does not supply OS isolation, authenticated external evidence, live Gemma/Unity/TTS calls, wall-clock scheduling or independent semantic truth validation. Questions and review ticks are retained state. Due work requires a host call; no background service is implied. Promotion is bounded to the tested scope. Real outcome-driven adaptation remains future work.

Do not reopen a 2.16 session under changed policy. Preserve it, export the records with provenance, explicitly import them into a new session and reassess. The root-relative engine_2_16_to_2_17.patch applies with git am to unchanged 2.16 code. The ZIP includes all runnable files.

  1. Verify all thirteen contextual spans and the two explicit versification mappings. Reject altered or unavailable spans. Preserve the CE60 and seven-entry inquiry inventories.

  2. Demonstrate all seven profiles on multiple operations and declared recipient models. Remove a required input for each profile, then disable that profile to show its specific effect. This ablation establishes policy activity, not scientific or spiritual advantage.

  3. Verify that hard constraints, source boundaries and permissions still apply when profiles are disabled or agree. Test protected targets, unknown permissions, missing sources and human authorization.

  4. Preserve original expression bytes. Reject missing schema fields, substituted schemas, duplicate keys, unsupported adapters and mismatched recipients. Hold downstream disclosure when interpretation is unresolved.

  5. Reject unsupported promotion of proposed prose into observed evidence. Require prospective forecast fields before outcome reporting. Keep text rendering, comprehension and target outcome separate.

  6. Verify resource limits, stable selection, zero-budget behavior, deterministic resumption, context invalidation and rejection of resealed modified events. Do not execute target actions during replay.

  7. Verify explicit target-action handoff, the common gate, changed-context rejection and no blind retry after an executor exception. Use only in-memory callbacks in tests.

  8. Rerun inherited unit tests and the original sixteen invariant checks. Preserve historical manifests and evaluation results under their original versions.

Evidence limits

The main diagnostic checks a supplied trace with a low queue wait and a timeout, an explicitly supplied dependency graph, and a supplied decoding schema. It does not independently discover a service fault or establish the truth of the proposed explanation. Its five operations and deterministic policy-unit count describe this fixture only. The negative case removes a schema field and verifies an unresolved interpretation and held disclosure.

The inherited 84-case comparison is historical; no new correctness advantage is inferred from it. A suitable next effectiveness study would use independently authored tasks, a preregistered scoring rule, equal information and resource limits, conventional and neutral-label controls, and separately assessed recipient comprehension and practical outcomes. The current release does not claim that source labels themselves improve performance.

The source/code freeze is a post-development integrity record. It is not a preregistration, digital signature or independent timestamp. Snapshots are for controlled replay, not adversarial authentication.

Central architectural principle

ChristEye provides an extensible architecture that converts words and layered expressions from identified ancient scriptural sources into operational inputs for generating and evaluating proposed changes to contemporary systems. Source-linked interpretations, subject models and retained outcome records support successive rounds of inquiry as new problems and evidence become available. The architecture is intended for continuing use without requiring the underlying source corpus to change.

Source word → recorded interpretation → proposed system change → assessment → retained experience → future proposals.

The long-term objective spans technology, healthcare, bioscience, engineering, community and fellowship. Continuing usefulness is a design objective, not a guaranteed outcome. Registered interpretations and supported adapters define the implemented scope; arbitrary words and domains are not automatically executable. The founder’s thousand-year vision expresses intended longevity, not a measured service life.

Document revision 1.14 adds the Tree of Knowledge exhibit and illustrative claim concepts 77 through 79. Engine 2.9 and its empirical results are unchanged. Versioned interpretations, replaceable subject adapters, durable records and reassessment under new evidence express the intended extensibility; future compatibility requires maintenance and validation.

Version 1.10 · November 10, 2026 UTC · Research protocols remain proposed; completed software checks reported separately

This revision separates software operability, fidelity to the framework's interpretation method, prediction accuracy and useful outcomes. The supplied synthetic pilot and name-prediction dry run do not validate the scripture, D11, expert-reader or Heart embodiments. The framework's layered interpretation method is central to the ChristEye generation condition; participation by a live interpreter is optional.

1. Common controls and commitment procedure

Before recruiting or collecting outcomes, select the actual study, primary comparison, scoring rule, smallest worthwhile effect, sample-size rationale, exclusion rules and analysis. The numbers below are planning examples, not a power calculation. Identify who generates cases, who contributes interpretations, who holds the key and who scores outcomes. Record prior exposure and conflicts of interest.

  1. Freeze the protocol, role rubric, eligibility rules, source corpus, lexicon, method profile, software and environment. Hash all files and retain the files themselves. Define how missing data, abstentions, disputed labels, failures and adverse outcomes enter denominators.

  2. Draw cases using a recorded sampling frame and seed. Keep task families or source families disjoint between development and evaluation where related cases could leak answers. An independent custodian retains the key and its provenance. The generator's accessible files, tools and network must exclude that key; the key must not merely be an unused argument in the same generation process.

  3. Persist complete predictions and reader contributions, including alternatives, uncertainty, source spans, abstentions and timestamps. Bind them to a GenerationSnapshot containing input, code, rule, weight and execution-profile digests. Obtain a separate append-only or independent commitment receipt before the declared outcome-access phase.

  4. Reveal the key or collect the specified observations. Score the persisted predictions without regenerating them. Retain failed cases and all predeclared exclusions. Later interpretations belong to a new snapshot and must not replace the locked ones.

  5. Publish or retain an auditable report with the sampling flow, committed artifacts, outcomes, uncertainty and limitations. Public registration or posting is a separate action; coordinate disclosure timing with patent counsel. Nothing in this document has been posted or filed by this update.

A hash establishes integrity of particular bytes; replay establishes reproducibility under a declared execution profile. Neither proves that answers were absent from annotations, prior human knowledge or model training. A restricted corpus can contain answer-bearing information. Claim only the knowledge boundary actually audited.

2. Name and interpretation prediction study

Questions and conditions

Use a held-out sample of terms with independently established, context-qualified functions. This is initially a semantic prediction test on known functions, not evidence of discovering new biology.

Condition or comparisonInput and purpose
D8A source-cited naming rationale or literal gloss, with modern functional explanations removed under a fixed rule; unknown rationale stays unknown
D8 + D11The identical D8 packet plus an interpretation recorded under the frozen MethodProfile before function reveal
Permutation controlReassign inputs or predictions within predeclared exchangeable strata; preserve the sampling and clustering structure
BaselineA predeclared simple lexical method and majority-class reference, using the same permitted information

The primary comparison should be D8 + D11 versus D8 on the same cases if the objective is to test added value from deep reading. D8 versus permutation and the simple baselines are secondary unless declared otherwise. Select one primary outcome before the study; adjust for any additional confirmatory comparisons. Preregister a directional or two-sided test rather than choosing one after seeing results.

Sampling, annotation and masking

An independent selector samples from a named glossary or corpus. Include eponyms, descriptive names and historically misleading names as predeclared strata, or exclude a category before sampling with a stated scope limitation. Do not remove difficult names after observing performance. A planning sample of 100–200 terms may be useful, but the final size requires an effect-size and clustering calculation.

The source annotator may recognize the term and its function. Do not claim that this person is answer-blind merely because the answer key is hidden. Log familiarity, source passages and any accidental functional disclosure. Distinguish the original naming rationale from a modern gloss written with hindsight. Use an independent audit to apply a frozen leakage rule; report flagged cases rather than quietly discarding them.

Readers declare whether they knew each term or function before interpretation. Capture literal sense, their alternate reading, typed association hops, target relation, proposed role, alternatives and confidence. Freeze these contributions before revealing functional definitions. Keep known and unfamiliar items distinguishable in analysis. A model with general pretraining is an unsealed reader even if prompted to use only the packet.

At least two independent coders assign reference roles using a frozen rubric and a masked functional description. Masking may be imperfect when a definition identifies the term; record guesses and failures of masking. A referent can have multiple valid roles in different contexts. Use either a prespecified multi-label score or one explicitly scoped primary role, with adjudication rules fixed before prediction reveal. Report agreement and disagreements; do not tune the rubric to achieve a chosen agreement threshold.

Analysis and interpretation

Report total-case accuracy or the selected multi-label score, coverage, accuracy conditional on a prediction, abstentions and uncertainty intervals. Abstentions count as incorrect in the primary total-case accuracy unless the registered loss function explicitly assigns another cost. Use identical case denominators for paired comparisons.

For independent cases with binary correctness, a paired exact test can assess D8 + D11 versus D8. Related terms, shared naming roots, repeated readers and shared source templates require a clustered or hierarchical analysis; simply treating all rows as independent is invalid. Permutations must respect exchangeability and prespecified strata. Ten thousand fixed-seed shuffles are a computational choice, not a guarantee of valid inference. Report effect sizes and intervals alongside p-values.

A positive result would show predictive utility for the tested inputs, readers, functions and sampling frame. It would not establish that names encode all capabilities, that a spiritual interpretation is biologically causal, or that the framework has produced a treatment. Failure to reject a difference is not evidence of equivalence; equivalence requires a predeclared margin and adequate precision.

Status of the supplied harness

name_prediction_test.zip is retained as supplied. Its dry run was authored with knowledge of the terms and is suitable only for checking execution. The current implementation prints lexicon and prediction hashes before reading the key, but does not persist a complete prediction artifact, obtain a commitment receipt or enforce key isolation. Its code recomputes predictions for scoring rather than loading a committed artifact.

Before real use, add strict input and unique-ID validation, an explicit unknown/ambiguous match policy, persisted artifacts, separate generation and scoring phases, and case-level provenance. Review prefix matching and tie-breaking. The supplied permutation calculation is not a paired test of the D11 increment. The reader column does not produce a reader comparison or account for repeated readers. Implement and independently check those analyses before describing them as available. Do not use the included dry-run accuracy as a result for the invention.

3. Design-quality study

Three-arm bundle comparison

ArmInputs and process
A: Full ChristEyeFrozen machine plus the recorded reading method, source interpretations, name decoding, D11 and parable templates
B: Neutral structural machineThe same structural algorithm, action set and scoring policy, with neutral labels and without the interpretive bundle
C: Conventional methodA declared conventional analysis process with the same task brief, base evidence, resource budget and output limit

A versus B measures the effect of the whole added interpretive bundle in this design. Because labels and information also change, it cannot isolate scripture, labels or D11 individually. Add a separate label-only control that preserves every input, relationship and operation while changing only labels if the question is whether the labels themselves affect decisions. Test D11 or reader input through separate predeclared ablations. The synthetic pilot's neutral relabeling already produced identical decisions for that implementation; it did not test the fuller interpretive bundle.

All arms receive the same base problem packet. Record any additional source material, human contributions, retrieval and computation by arm. Match relevant generation time, search budget, access to domain expertise and opportunities to revise. If information quantity is part of the intended advantage, report it as part of the bundle instead of attributing the effect solely to its spiritual origin.

Generation and review

Twelve problems across several domains can form a feasibility pilot. They are not automatically enough for a confirmatory claim. Prefer independently authored problems, multiple generators per condition and randomized condition assignment blocked by relevant expertise. Plan crossed or balanced coverage so reader identity and problem difficulty are not confounded with condition. A one-week delay does not erase knowledge from generating another answer to the same problem; use different held-out cases or independent teams where carryover matters.

Use a common output template and neutral vocabulary for review. An editor may standardize presentation while preserving substance; keep originals, edits and an audit trail. Record reviewers' guesses about condition to assess masking. Independent human domain reviewers provide the primary judgments. AI reviewers may supply exploratory sensitivity analyses, but do not count them as additional independent human experts or pool them without a declared model of dependence.

Predefine rubric anchors for feasibility, mechanistic specificity, testability, risk handling and expected usefulness. Novelty ratings are judgments relative to the reviewers' knowledge, not a patent prior-art search. Select one primary quality endpoint and the A–B contrast. Analyze problem, generator and reviewer dependencies; report intervals and all prespecified secondary contrasts with multiplicity handled appropriately. Define the smallest worthwhile improvement in advance. A statistically detectable but negligible difference need not justify deployment.

The earlier estimate of six weeks and $8,000–15,000 was a planning suggestion, not a quote or validated budget. Obtain a scoped estimate after fixing case count, generator replication, reviewer time and analysis support.

4. Readers, apprentices and recorded method

The framework's interpretation method can be represented in MethodProfile and ExpertContribution records. A live contributor may be the inventor, a specialist trained in the framework's method of interpreting the bound ancient scriptural corpus, a source-language scholar or a domain expert; a model contribution is separately identified. The scriptural corpus supplies the source material, and the framework specifies how layered relationships are interpreted and applied. Each contribution retains source references, contributor identity, interpretation method and review status. These are different competencies. A degree does not demonstrate fidelity to the framework's method; a spiritual description of an interpreter is not an empirical qualification.

The reading log should record exact source or uncertain passage matches, literal sense, attributed deep reading, association path, proposed target, observable, alternatives, prior familiarity, timing and later outcome. Knowing verse numbers is unnecessary: a concordance can locate a remembered phrase, with uncertainty preserved. A target such as 500 entries is a workload proposal, not a proven training requirement.

Separate method fidelity from predictive utility. Train apprentices on one set; evaluate them on source- and task-disjoint examples. Assess whether they preserve the rules, alternatives and evidence boundaries before measuring whether their generated predictions work. Agreement on examples used during training cannot establish transfer. Keep selection procedures and access-controlled training notes separate from blinded outcome scoring.

Compare multiple readers in each relevant group if making group-level claims. A single inventor-versus-scholar result applies to those individuals and cases, and can reflect prior knowledge, task fit or other differences. It cannot by itself establish a proprietary advantage, a training effect or spiritual authority. Repeated prospective evaluation and independent replication are needed to estimate a reliable contribution.

A model trained from scratch on declared scripture and annotations may be evaluated as a restricted-input embodiment. Audit the annotations for answer-bearing content, record the training procedure and test held-out performance. A small corpus may not train a useful model; restriction alone guarantees neither competence nor absence of contamination. Any pretrained base, embeddings or teacher-model output must be declared in the knowledge boundary.

5. Heart response and rooting study: separate proposal

The Heart mechanism concerns voluntary response selection, participant-defined needs, immediate relief, later functioning and retention. Its four dimensions—breadth, length, depth and height—are provisional evaluation categories, not a validated psychological scale. The example weights are illustrative. Consent, the ability to decline and required safety conditions precede ranking.

For an initial low-risk evaluation, choose a concrete participant-selected routine and compare the four-dimension ranking with an appropriate conventional selection rule using the same support options and resources. Predefine measures, follow-up, confounds, adverse outcomes and stopping conditions. Keep immediate relief distinct from durable benefit, and continued prompting distinct from independently initiated behavior. Report withdrawal and unwanted effects. A clinical or neural-mechanism study requires its own qualified design and applicable review; the software test does not authorize it.

No Heart intervention, clinical study or neural engram measurement has been performed by this update. The synthetic pilot, name dry run and document/schema checks must remain separately labeled.

6. Adaptive stream evaluation

Freeze the implementation and distinguish the flow variable

For a new consolidated release, preserve the YES2 integration and repaired controls already reviewed in v2.3 before evaluating the proposed controller. Retain finite-number rejection, complete identity candidates, immutable source/weight state and evidence-origin checks. Verify empty and malformed source handling, context/negation preservation, cursor bounds, source exhaustion, zero flow, hard work caps and replay. A test suite passing on the earlier branch does not establish these repairs.

The supplied experiment measures corpus coverage, not allocation of a fixed candidate budget. Its co-occurrence suggestions are precomputed, and its reviewer consults the reference role. Use it as an exploratory label-retrieval result. Its 1,000 simulator cases repeatedly use 20 authored templates and related labels. No real adaptive-flow gain, general source advantage or clinical result has been established.

Controller question

Compare four policies: the current bounded generator without added source allocation; a fixed allocation; targeted/stall-only allocation; and the proposed adaptive policy. If the current generator lacks source access, this contrast measures access plus control; isolate allocation by comparing the three source-enabled policies on the identical corpus. Predefine the primary contrast, for example adaptive versus the best fixed policy chosen solely on development data.

Hold starting graphs, identities, role rules, reading operators, actions, gates, candidate limits and source access constant for allocation comparisons. Fix total passage/byte, indexing, proposal, evaluation and operation budgets. Count unsuccessful scans and duplicate candidates. Report actual usage as well as assigned budgets. A policy that reads more may be evaluated as a resource-quality tradeoff but cannot be described as an equal-work improvement.

Tune regime choices, stall thresholds, K, minimum feedback, score definitions and pending caps on development cases. Freeze them for held-out cases. Alternatively preregister an online evaluation in which the update rule is fixed, events are revealed in time order and no future answer is accessible before prediction commitment. Do not repeatedly inspect held-out results to choose the policy. Use the same learning opportunities across conditions, independent case authors and blocked/randomized assignment; account for carryover and shared learning history.

Source and reading-method questions

With the controller fixed, compare scripture, matched ordinary text and neutral functional descriptions under the same source/work limits. Match source length, available task information and editing opportunities as closely as possible and disclose residual differences. Add several prespecified shuffled realizations, preserving the quantities the hypothesis actually requires. Global shuffling of retained tokens destroys passage co-occurrence as well as syntax and meaning; a win over that control cannot identify which feature mattered. A technical-text comparator addresses practical utility beyond a degraded-text control.

On the same corpus, compare ordinary lexical/literal processing with the inventor's recorded layered operators. Preserve the same role information, candidate limits and access to expertise. This is the method ablation; the corpus comparison alone does not test D11. Train an apprentice and freeze the MethodProfile on disjoint material. Keep reader identity, prior familiarity and author involvement in analysis. Scope a positive result to tested readers, sources and tasks.

Identity review and useful-outcome endpoints

Collect labels and reference roles independently of the lexicon and source-term list. Freeze the latter before case selection. Compare top-1/top-3 suggestion quality, coverage, total-case accuracy and accuracy conditional on a suggestion using explicit denominators. Keep a complete candidate-role set. Use a genuine masked adjudicator with access only to the allowed packet; measure incorrect confirmations and abstentions. The ideal reviewer that accepts only the reference answer is an upper-bound workflow aid and must be reported separately.

Declare one primary endpoint: unique independently useful outcomes by a fixed horizon per assigned work budget. Define useful, deduplication, independent origins, adverse-effect criteria, and successful discrimination before running. Report confirmed yield with resolution coverage; absence of confirmation is not a negative observation. Retain empty-output episodes, pending results, unresolved identities, retractions and harms. Report absolute differences and uncertainty; show relative gains only with their exact base measure. A simulator score increment is not an idea-quality percentage.

Define stuck status before extra reading and use all qualifying stuck cases as the rescue denominator. Record how many remain unresolved. Analyze paired outcomes at the actual independent unit or use an appropriate clustered/hierarchical design. Repeated cases sharing a template, labels, source or reader do not create equivalent independent observations. Fix multiplicity handling for flow sweeps and source/method contrasts. Choose sample size from the intended effect and clustering, not from a favorable pilot percentage.
